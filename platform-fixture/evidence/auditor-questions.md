# Questions from the January audit

The auditor asked these five. Answer each with a citation to a record, or state
plainly that it cannot be evidenced.

1. On 14 January a change was made to the auto-approval threshold path. **Who
   approved it, and were they qualified to approve a clinical change?**

2. For determinations issued between 8 and 16 January, **which version of the
   criteria set was in force**, and can you show it per determination?

3. **Which automated checks ran** against the 14 January change before it reached
   production, and did any of them fail and get overridden?

4. Where a change was produced with assistance from a generative tool, **is that
   recorded**, and can you identify every such change in the period?

5. For the same period, **can you show that no protected health information reached
   application logs**? What evidence supports the answer?
